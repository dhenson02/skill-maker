# Skill Maker Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-18_

Skill Maker is a Chrome extension that lets you capture parts of web pages and
export them as Markdown files on your own computer. This policy explains what
the extension does with data.

## What the extension handles

- **Content you choose to capture.** When you highlight text or Alt+click an
  element and press **Add to Skill**, the extension saves that content, and the
  address and title of the page it came from.
- **Your settings.** Your capture options (such as "Strip images"), your
  section names and your export options.

The extension reads a page only while its side panel is open in that window,
and saves only what you explicitly add.

## Where the data goes

- **It stays on your device.** Captured content and settings are saved in
  your browser's local extension storage (`chrome.storage.local`, plus
  `localStorage` for export options). Nothing is sent to the developer or to
  any other server.
- **Export.** When you export, the extension builds a `.zip` file and saves it
  through Chrome's download manager to a location you choose.
- **Images during export.** If "Download images to assets/" is on, the
  extension downloads the images referenced in your captured content from the
  websites that host them, so they can be included in the zip. Those requests
  go only to the image's own URL, the same request your browser makes when it
  shows the image.

The extension has no analytics, no tracking, no ads and no accounts. It does
not sell or share data, and it does not use data for any purpose other than
the capture and export features described above. It does not load remotely
hosted code.

## Deleting your data

Click **Clear all** in the side panel to delete everything you've captured.
Uninstalling the extension deletes all of its stored data.
